01 · Win work · Live project search

Strategic Project Discovery searches live procurement sources

The problem
Business development teams check the same portals by hand, one sector at a time.
What I built
A scanner across 676 procurement sources in seven sectors. Each result is plotted on a map with a fit assessment.

02 · Win work · Bid-intelligence database

GrowthSignal collects public tenders and programmes into one database

The problem
Opportunities are spread across hundreds of procurement portals, development bank registers and news sources. Teams often find them late.
What I built
A self-hosted database of about 58,000 tenders and programmes from over 270 public sources. Claude tags each record with sector, stage, value and location, and the full set is searchable.
Open the live tool ↗

Two tiers of bid intelligence

How the two business development tools compare

Strategic Project Discovery runs a live search when a team needs one. GrowthSignal keeps a database for teams that track opportunities every day.

CriterionLive project searchStrategic Project DiscoveryBid-intelligence databaseGrowthSignal
How it worksGemini with Google Search runs a live search across 676 procurement sources on each scan.Connectors collect about 58,000 tenders and programmes from 270+ public sources on every update run.
SetupNone. It needs one web page and an API key.A server, a SQLite database and a Claude login.
CoverageAnything web search can find, including portals without a connector.Connected sources only, but complete, repeatable and de-duplicated.
MemoryEach scan stands alone and saves as a JSON file.Full history, with record updates, notes and duplicate merging.
RankingA fit assessment per result covering sector, scale and geography, with an estimated win probability.Sector fit, project stage, value and corroborating sources, set against a firm’s target sectors, stages and countries.
Best forA business development lead before a pipeline meeting.A business development team running its pipeline every day.

03 · Design · BIM model queries

ModelChat answers plain-English questions about a BIM model

The problem
Getting answers out of a BIM model usually needs a specialist and a desktop licence.
What I built
A browser IFC viewer with a chat panel. Claude answers by querying the model index and operating the viewer: isolate, highlight, section, measure.

04 · Design · Energy modelling

Reference Buildings Explorer shows energy model results in 3D

The problem
Energy model results sit in spreadsheets and HTML reports that are hard to compare.
What I built
The US DOE commercial reference buildings, 16 types in 16 climates, with EnergyPlus geometry and results in six interactive 3D views.

How I turned public DOE data into a research tool

  1. 01 · SourceOne US Department of Energy web page with broken model links.
  2. 02 · Recovery64 downloads checked and 864 source files organised.
  3. 03 · Dataset256 building and climate combinations, with geometry and simulation results.
  4. 04 · OutputSix interactive views in one browser file.

05 · Design · Material compliance

MaterialScan checks materials against three standards

The problem
Certification evidence for each product sits in spreadsheets, PDFs and email.
What I built
A dashboard that scores each product against LBC 4.1, WELL v2 and the client brief, with the relevant clause and evidence alongside.

  1. People

    Tailored capability programme

    I establish a baseline through a team survey and leadership interviews, then design an enablement day and three practical labs around the team’s real work. Pulse surveys and office hours support adoption afterwards.

    3. Labs
    4. Support

    You receiveA baseline survey, an enablement day, practical labs and adoption support.

    Workflow diagnostic

    2. Map

    4. Plan

    Prototype sprint

    I build one narrowly defined use case with agreed data and users, then test whether it works in practice.
